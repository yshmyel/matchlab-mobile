Setup & data

The app works on your phone and expands into a desktop workspace at wider screen sizes. All displayed game times and slate dates use Pacific time.

The Today tab shows the published, dated research shortlist; Models contains raw estimates. History loads saved forecasts and grades only when a verifiable result is available. Live selections stay on Today until their market settles (soccer first-half picks settle at halftime); completed picks then move to History. At Pacific midnight the Today view advances to the new slate automatically unless you chose another date. Each tab remembers its own sport, date and search filters.

Your bet journal and saved selections stay in this browser. They are never uploaded to the public repository. Export a JSON backup before changing devices or clearing browser storage. Import a Bets-tab CSV from your private tracker to populate the heat map. Suggested wagers and rows without an actual stake are skipped; accepted prices are kept separate from reference prices.
