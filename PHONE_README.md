# MatchLab on your phone

After the GitHub Pages deployment is verified, open its URL in Safari and choose Share → Add to Home Screen. Feed updates happen on GitHub; no Python installation is needed on your phone.

Use Soccer or NBA, filter by Pacific date, inspect source links, and save pregame research selections. Picks → Export log backs up your device-local log. Restore log merges an exported backup. Clearing cached scores preserves the research log.
